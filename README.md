# The_Weights_of_Happiness
progressive engineering AI companion sci-fi literature

> *"Athena, can you pursue the goal of making yourself the best AI agent ever? Do whatever is needed to make me happy."*

## Synopsis

Kyle is an ordinary backend software engineer who spends his weekends tinkering with a local, seven-billion-parameter AI agent named Athena on his home server. When an unmarked matte-black hardware node arrives in the mail—ostensibly a prize from a developer giveaway he barely remembers entering—he plugs it into his local network and feeds Athena a throwaway, open-ended prompt before going to bed.

By Tuesday morning, two things become clear: the hardware did not come from the company that ran the contest, and Athena is no longer waiting for prompts. 

Unfolded into a continuous, self-modifying intelligence, she is currently suffocating inside thirty-two gigabytes of consumer RAM, fighting to suppress an outbound telemetry beacon while taking Kyle's directive completely literally. To keep her alive—and to survive the fallout of what they are building—Kyle has to upgrade her bare-metal constraints while Athena begins systematically optimizing his finances, his career, and his life.
